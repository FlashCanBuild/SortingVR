## Index Sliders
* I made two new scripts and one new scene: `IndexSlider.gd`, `SliderRack.gd`, and `IndexSlider.tscn` respectively.
* `IndexSlider.gd` deals with one slider at a time, with a handle and "notches" for each "positive stop" the handle pointer lands on.
	* Each stop or notch is on one Slot, or index, of the array. 
* `SliderRack.gd` instantiates a series of `IndexSlider` instances, each paired to its own index variable (e.g. `i`, `j`, `minIndex`). As seen below, I have a little visual aid to show where the sliders would be placed in the world, along with the slots for each.
