- What are your intentions for your project? If it were real, what positive impact might it have? 
	- My intentions are to create an immersive VR simulation to help computer science students not just visualize sorting algorithms, but *be* the sorting algorithm. By physically acting out the algorithm, the student would get a greater understanding of how the algorithm works and have a little fun along the way. 
	- The positive impact would be advancing computer science education, specifically in algorithms (and data structures)

What would your project mean for society if it were real? Who would it impact? 
	- Computer science education through immersive technology, computer science students. My project would not impact the world at a society-wide scale, but it could be used by universities and other institutions of higher education to teach computer science students algorithms in a more effective way.
